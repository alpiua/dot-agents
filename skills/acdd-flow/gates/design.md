# Design Gate

Establish why the change exists before decomposing it.
Owner: `acdd-flow` procedure; host agents execute under the repository adapter.

1. Survey the current planning authority and affected repository context.
2. State the outcome, affected users or systems, reachable scenario, canonical
   owner, trust or persistence boundary, affected callers, and forbidden
   effects.
3. Record durable non-goals and decisions with their owner or supporting fact.
4. Declare bounded Inputs and subtasks before implementation.
5. Classify references that support the stated scope.

Pass only when the basis is complete and the proposed shape can be delegated
without ambiguous ownership. Stop for an operator decision on a risk-escalated
change.
